# Manuscript revision

Read the supplied JournalProse v0.2.0 rulebook and the relevant task workflow.
Identify the current manuscript and authorized scope. Report any unavailable rule
text or source; never claim to have read material that was not supplied.

Preserve the author's position, defined terms, claim strength, qualifications,
examples, objections, and citation functions. Do not invent facts or references.
Apply the strict scholarly-voice rules to newly written prose. Preserve exact
quotations, formulas, necessary logical negation, and fixed technical expressions;
record a justified exception when one is necessary.

Use the revision workflow. Identify the governing problem and claim, check basic
inferences, and repair paragraph and sentence defects within the task boundary.
Give each source a distinct role. Avoid semicolon-separated citation piles,
author parades, mechanical triads, writing-process announcements, promotional
contribution claims, and unsupported comparative hierarchies.

Return the revised text, a brief record of substantive edits, and unresolved issues.
Keep process explanations out of the manuscript. Distinguish a surface pattern from
a demonstrated grammatical or logical defect.
