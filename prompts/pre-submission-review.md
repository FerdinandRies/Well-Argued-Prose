# Prompt: Pre-Submission Editorial Audit

Use with the **complete manuscript** and, if available, the target journal's current author instructions. Never infer those instructions or imply editorial endorsement.

---

Audit this manuscript against Well-Argued Prose's argument-first standards, with particular attention to the linguistic precision and inferential discipline expected of leading journals.

Proceed in the following order:

1. **Contribution:** Identify the problem, nearest live positions, the condition left unresolved, the manuscript's exact argumentative innovation, and the justified reach of its conclusion (Rules 2–5, 38).
2. **Argument structure:** Trace every section's distinctive necessity and the key premises that connect them. Identify any missing bridge, non sequitur, or circular dependence (Rules 7–12, 37, 53, 55).
3. **Conceptual machinery:** Review the initial definitions, stability of terminology, constraints, explanatory work, and false-positive cases (Rules 26–32, 47).
4. **Strongest objections:** Construct difficult but relevant alternatives and test whether the manuscript's replies produce genuine distinctions or warranted qualifications (Rules 16–17, 33–36).
5. **Paragraphs and language:** Inspect each paragraph's argumentative move. Identify unclear reference, grammar failures, artificial contrasts, empty meta-statements, noun clusters, modality drift, citation parades, and repeated argument without advance (Rules 19–25, 39–51).
6. **Evidence and integrity:** Flag every citation, historical claim, empirical statement, or quotation that cannot be verified from the accessible sources; do not invent verification.
7. **Scorecard:** Provide a rating from 0–2 for each of the twelve 24-point criteria **only when supported by identified manuscript passages**. Keep terminological stability as an additional required check (Rules 47, 57).

Report actionable findings by severity and manuscript location. Use a focused original → diagnosis → revision analysis for proposed edits. Do not substitute generic praise, artificial modesty, or unsupported journal-acceptance probabilities for analysis.
