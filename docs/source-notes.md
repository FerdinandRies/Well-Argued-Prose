# Source and Editorial Notes

This project adapts a 57-rule advanced academic writing manuscript for public release. The rule hierarchy, editorial standards, revision actions, twelve-paper *Mind* reference corpus, and twelve-criterion human scorecard remain intact.

## Published-article examples

All 57 examples are newly authored explanatory reconstructions grounded in named, published articles from *Mind*. They are **not verbatim quotations** and must never be attributed to the original authors as their exact wording. Each entry carries stable source IDs linked to the corresponding DOI in the generated handbook and [example source map](example-sources.md). The repository redistributes no full-text articles or proprietary figures.

For texts restricted to publisher abstracts or extracts, the cases follow the publicly available account and the supplied comparative study, without claiming passage-level verification of inaccessible full text. The examples are editorial learning aids: the source authors have neither written these sentences nor endorsed this tool. Original publisher pages are the authoritative source for checking the precise language of a passage.

## Editorial corrections preserved

- The supplied task index had several rule-number mismatches. The public [workflows](workflows.md) use verified rule IDs.
- The original 24-point scorecard and prose specification differed over a scored criterion. The public scorecard consistently follows the twelve appendix criteria. Terminology stability is checked separately under Rule 47.
- Numeric suggestions such as a 350-word opening and a 60-word short paragraph are editorial prompts, not publisher requirements.

## Publication boundaries

Bibliographic metadata and DOIs are linked to the official Oxford Academic pages. The reference commentary and examples are interpretive reconstructions, not journal policy, peer-reviewed validation of the tool, or a representation of the original article text.
