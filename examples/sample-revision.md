# A hypothetical record audit

All names, studies, years, and numerical details below are invented for demonstration.
The prose illustrates targeted repairs; it reports no empirical research.

The catalog links each record to its location. Evaluating the catalog requires
checking whether its entries correctly describe the records and whether the required
records are present.

The register lists six rejected applications. Assessing the fairness of those
rejections also requires the eligibility criteria and the reasons given for each
decision. The count alone leaves that assessment unresolved.

Missing entries affect the cases represented in the register (Author A 2001).
Errors within recorded values affect accuracy even when the required entries are
present (Author B 2002). The two sources address separate defects in this hypothetical
literature example.

Every funded project is audited. An audit alone leaves Project K's funding status
unresolved because unfunded projects may also be audited.
